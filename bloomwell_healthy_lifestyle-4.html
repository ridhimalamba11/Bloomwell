Build a balanced plate: aim for plenty of vegetables and fruit, include grains or other starchy foods, and add protein foods such as lentils, beans, dairy, eggs, tofu or nuts. Adapt portions to your age, activity, culture, appetite and health needs.

🌈 Eat a variety

  • Choose different-coloured vegetables and fruits.
  • Include pulses, beans or other protein foods.
  • Prefer whole grains often when available.
  • Limit frequent sugary drinks and very salty packaged snacks.

💧 Water reminder

Drink water regularly, especially in hot weather or during activity. Needs vary with age, activity, climate and health—follow any clinician's fluid advice.

A flexible example—adjust the times to your own day.

🏃 Move in a way you enjoy

Walking, dancing, cycling, yoga, sports or active play all count. Start at a comfortable level and build gradually. Children and adults have different activity needs.

🌙 Make room for rest

Try a quiet activity before bed, keep your sleep space comfortable and reduce late-night screen use if it affects your sleep. Sleep needs depend on age.

Personal Hygiene

Simple ways to keep yourself and your surroundings clean.

👐 Hand hygiene

🪥 Body and oral care

🥣 Food hygiene

  • Wash produce under clean running water.
  • Keep raw and cooked foods separate.
  • Cook food thoroughly and refrigerate perishables promptly.
  • Use clean utensils and safe drinking water.

🏡 Clean surroundings

  • Put rubbish in a covered bin and empty it regularly.
  • Keep bathrooms and frequently touched surfaces clean.
  • Keep stagnant water from collecting around the home.
  • Allow rooms to air out when safe and practical.
Hygiene is about regular, practical habits—not needing to be perfect. Use products as directed and avoid harsh products that irritate your skin.

Self-Care & Mindful Living

Care for your mind, body and confidence with small moments of kindness.

🌼

Pause and breathe

Take a few slow, comfortable breaths and relax your shoulders.

📓

Write it down

Note one thing you feel grateful for or one goal for tomorrow.

🎨

Enjoy a hobby

Read, draw, listen to music, garden or do something you love.

💛

Stay connected

Talk to someone you trust and ask for support when you need it.

🌻 Your 5-minute reset

  1. Put your phone aside for a moment.
  2. Relax your jaw and shoulders.
  3. Breathe slowly without forcing it.
  4. Notice what you can see, hear and feel.
  5. Choose one small next step.

🌱 Healthy boundaries

  • Take breaks when you need them.
  • Speak to yourself as kindly as you would to a friend.
  • Make time for people and activities that support you.
  • Ask a trusted person or professional for help if stress feels too heavy.
Like a sunflower turns toward the light, give yourself permission to look for little moments of hope, rest and joy. 🌻
